Thesis

My thesis explores possible applications of my internship paper (linked in the Dowker Persistence tile!). We are looking at server-load and telemetry data that includes CPU load, memory usage, and network packet rate to build a markov chain where we could identify if normal load or traffic have a stable cycle. On the other hand, does overloaded networks or a DDoS attack widely disrupt the cycle? Do the birth/death times and shifts in those metrics clearly help identify attacks or anomalies in the system? This would focus on understanding what the data shape tells us instead of just reading the statistics of it.
